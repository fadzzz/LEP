  <div class="lead">

    Bouquet and monthly rente scale with senior age at acquisition. Younger seniors get a smaller bouquet and lower monthly because LEP carries the property longer. Older seniors get a larger bouquet and higher monthly because LEP recovers capital sooner. The total family benefit lands in the same range ($278K to $293K) across all three tiers, just delivered on a different timeline.

  </div>


  <div class="principle">

    <strong>Underwriting principle:</strong> bouquet and monthly are calibrated so the senior's family receives roughly equivalent total value regardless of age, while LEP's profit margin per property remains consistent across tiers ($200K to $230K after the 15% heir payout). This prevents agents from cherry-picking older seniors and protects pipeline diversity.

  <div class="notes">

    <div class="notes-label">Notes & Caveats</div>

    <ol>

      <li><strong>Heir payout at 15%</strong> is positioned as a launch promotion for the first 6 to 12 months. Rolls back to 10% after the promo period. Gives sales agents a "limited time" hook with seniors and their families.</li>

      <li><strong>Occupancy figures are medians,</strong> not means, drawn from Quebec life expectancy data adjusted for senior cohort. Actual exits vary, the portfolio averages out across 100+ properties.</li>

      <li><strong>Per-property underwriting</strong> should also factor property condition, location, and senior health (if disclosed). These tier numbers are the baseline, with adjustments at the agent's discretion within a defined range.</li>

      <li><strong>Heirs as fractional investors</strong> is layered on top of the heir payout. Heirs can buy fractional shares of the family property at the same retail price as outside investors, keeping a financial connection to the home and giving LEP a free inbound investor channel.</li>

      <li><strong>Marketing line:</strong> "LEP Family First Program," combining the heir payout plus heir investor priority. The only fractional platform in Quebec that pays out to the senior's family at exit.</li>

    </ol>
